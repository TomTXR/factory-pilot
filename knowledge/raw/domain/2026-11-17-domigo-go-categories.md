Assessments are organized into three value-oriented categories:

| Category | Focus | Examples |
|----------|-------|----------|
| **GO Safer** | Safety & compliance | Fire safety, electrical safety, structural concerns, permit status |
| **GO Cheaper** | Cost optimization | Energy efficiency, insurance optimization, preventive maintenance |
| **GO Better** | Value enhancement | Property improvements, smart home integration, lifestyle upgrades |
