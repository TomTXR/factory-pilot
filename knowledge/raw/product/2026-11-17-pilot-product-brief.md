# Home Maintenance Log: Product Brief

> Source: Factory spike team, written for the factory-pilot app
> Collected: 2026-11-17
> Published: 2026-11-17
> Synthetic: yes

The Home Maintenance Log helps private home owners keep track of what needs maintenance in their home and what it cost.

Owners register their properties (a house or an apartment). For each property they list items that need care: heating, roof, windows, smoke detectors, appliances. For each item they log maintenance entries with date, description, cost in EUR and who did the work.

Goals for the first version:
- An owner can see at a glance what was maintained when and what it cost.
- Maintenance entries are grouped by the DomiGO value categories GO Safer, GO Cheaper and GO Better, so owners see where their money goes.
- Works well on a phone, because owners log entries on site.

Out of scope for the first version: user accounts and login, reminders, document uploads, sharing with tenants or contractors, multiple currencies.

Users are home owners in the Netherlands and Germany. Amounts are in EUR. Dates are shown in day-month-year order.
