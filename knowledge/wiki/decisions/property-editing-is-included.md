# Editing a property is part of the first version

> Sources: Story #20, product owner answer 6 on https://github.com/TomTXR/factory-pilot/issues/20#issuecomment-6980020998
> Raw: [Story 20 properties](../../raw/stories/2026-11-18-story-20-manage-properties.md)
> Status: accepted
> Updated: 2026-11-19

## Context

The first plan proposed no editing in this story.

## Decision

The product owner reversed the default: "no, we should add editing as well this is expected by a user". Add, switch, view, edit and delete are all in scope.

## Reason

Users expect to correct a property. The reason is the product owner's own words above.

## Alternatives

- Delete and re-add instead of editing: rejected by the product owner.
