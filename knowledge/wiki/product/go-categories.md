## Overview

DomiGO groups property work into three value categories. The pilot app uses them to classify items and maintenance costs.

| Category | Focus | Examples |
|----------|-------|----------|
| GO Safer | Safety and compliance | Fire safety, electrical safety, structural concerns, permit status |
| GO Cheaper | Cost optimization | Energy efficiency, insurance optimization, preventive maintenance |
| GO Better | Value enhancement | Property improvements, smart home integration, lifestyle upgrades |

## Use in the pilot app

- Each item has exactly one category (product brief, SYNTHETIC).
- Totals are shown per category, so owners see how much went to safety versus improvements (interview Anna, SYNTHETIC).
- Examples from the interview: smoke detectors and the boiler check are GO Safer.

## Open question

Preventive maintenance appears under GO Cheaper in DomiGO, but a boiler service is also a safety check. The sources do not say which category wins for such items. Ask the product owner before deciding.
