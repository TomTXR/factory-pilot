# Home Owner Persona

> Sources: Synthetic interview, 2026-11-17 (SYNTHETIC)
> Raw: [Interview Anna](../../raw/users/2026-11-17-interview-owner-anna.md); [Story 34 Google sign-in](../../raw/stories/2026-11-19-story-34-google-sign-in.md)
> Updated: 2026-11-19

## Overview

Private home owner in the Netherlands or Germany who maintains a family home and wants to remember what was done and what it cost. SYNTHETIC persona for the factory spike.

## Needs

- Log work on site, on a phone, quickly.
- Know when something was last serviced (installers ask).
- Yearly cost overview for the household budget, on a laptop.
- See safety-related spending separately from improvements.
- Log self-done work with cost 0.

## Constraints

- In the synthetic interview the persona wanted no account or login ("It is just for us"). The product owner decided on 2026-11-19 (#28, story #34) that the app requires Google sign-in with automatic, open sign-up and 30-day sessions; the stored Google email and name are accepted. The persona's wish remains a reason to keep sign-in to one click.
- Euros only.
